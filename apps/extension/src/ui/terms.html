2. What Envoy does

Envoy uses artificial intelligence to generate responses, so it cannot work without processing your data. This includes the text of your chats in the conversation you delegate, including messages written by other people. Envoy can only use messages currently loaded in the Messenger tab, and it does not upload your other chats. When you start a task, Envoy:

  • reads the messages loaded in the conversation you select;
  • sends them, together with your goal, to our backend;
  • keeps sending the loaded conversation at short intervals while the task is active and the tab is connected;
  • has our backend ask Meta's Model API, an artificial intelligence service, what to do when the task starts, when you change the instructions or resume, and when it detects a new or edited message.

Based on the model's output, Envoy may wait, ask you a question, finish the task, or prepare a message and send it in your name. Envoy can write first, even if the chat is empty or you sent the last message.

Auto-send is on by default. A prepared message is sent automatically after a 10-second countdown unless you edit it, cancel it, pause the task, or take over. You can change the delay to between 1 and 600 seconds, or turn auto-send off so that messages wait for Send now. A task keeps running while you use other tabs, as long as its Messenger tab stays open.
