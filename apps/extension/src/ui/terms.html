2. What Envoy does

Envoy uses artificial intelligence to generate responses, so it cannot work without processing your data. This includes the text of your chats in the conversation you delegate, including messages written by other people. When you start a task, Envoy:

  • reads the messages currently loaded in the Messenger conversation you select;
  • passes them, together with your goal, instructions, and answers, to the backend on your computer;
  • has the backend send this content to Meta's Model API, an artificial intelligence service, using your key.

Based on the model's output, Envoy may wait, ask you a question, propose a reply, or send a reply in your name. Unless you turn off auto-send, a proposed reply is sent automatically when its countdown ends.
