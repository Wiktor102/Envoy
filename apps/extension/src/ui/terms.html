2. What Envoy does

When you start a task, Envoy:

  • reads the messages currently loaded in the Messenger conversation you select;
  • sends them, together with your goal, instructions, and answers, to our backend;
  • has our backend send this content to Meta's Model API, an artificial intelligence service.

Based on the model's output, Envoy may wait, ask you a question, propose a reply, or send a reply in your name. Unless you turn off auto-send, a proposed reply is sent automatically when its countdown ends.
